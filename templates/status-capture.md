# Status capture — one block per job status

Operative statuses are a **separate concept** — use their own section below,
never merge the two.

```text
Name:                  <as displayed>
Internal id:           <where visible; else "not visible">
Description/help:      <verbatim, or "none">
Default status:        yes | no | not visible
Active:                yes | no | not visible
Ordering:              <position/sequence where meaningful>
Colour/icon:           <where meaningful>
Operative equivalent:  yes | no | unknown
Actions available FROM this status:   <list>
Actions that move jobs INTO it:       <list>
Expiry/timer behaviour:               <where visible>
Restrictions:                         <where visible>
Confidence:            VERIFIED — OBSERVED | VERIFIED — STRUCTURAL | INFERRED | UNKNOWN
Evidence:              E-NNN (evidence/<path>)
```
