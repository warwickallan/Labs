# Field catalogue entry — Action configurator (and similar screens)

One block per control. Inspect only — never edit. Unknown meaning is valid;
do not attempt to explain every field.

```text
Label:        <visible label>
Section:      <tab / section it lives in>
Control:      <text | dropdown | checkbox | radio | date | multi-select | ...>
Default:      <current/default value as shown>
Options:      <selectable options, verbatim; "n/a" for free text>
DOM name/id:  <where accessible without editing>
Conditional:  <yes/no; what makes it appear/disappear, where observable>
Help text:    <verbatim, or "none">
Meaning:      <probable meaning — plainly marked as probable>
Confidence:   VERIFIED — OBSERVED | VERIFIED — STRUCTURAL | INFERRED | UNKNOWN
Evidence:     E-NNN (evidence/<path>)
```
